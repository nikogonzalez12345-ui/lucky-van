LUCKY VANDrive the van through zombie country. Gamble your way to better guns.

How to play

Driver: WASD / arrow keys, Space to handbrake. Ram zombies for cash.
Everyone: aim with the mouse, hold left-click to shoot. Q or 1–6 swaps guns.

Reach the stop at the end of the road. At the stop, pool cash into van upgrades, buy guns and ammo, or bet it all at the van's casino. Everyone hits READY to roll out.

If the van's hull hits zero, the run is over.
